    How to use the Soundboard

    1. 1

      Connect the bot

      Pick a server and voice channel, then press Join. The status dot above turns green once the bot is in.

    2. 2

      Play a sound

      Press any sound button under Your sounds. It plays instantly in the voice channel.

    3. 3

      Search MyInstants

      Type a keyword and Search. Play it once, or Save it so it becomes a permanent button on your board.

    4. 4

      Add your own sound

      Drag audio files onto the Add your own sound box, or click to pick a file from your device.

    5. 5

      Stop & leave

      Stop halts the sound currently playing. Leave makes the bot exit the voice channel.

    Or via Discord chat

    Type these directly in a text channel (you must be in a voice channel):

    • sb! <keyword> Play the top MyInstants sound
    • sb!list <keyword> Show the top 5 results
    • sb!local <name> Play a saved sound
    • sb!sounds List saved sounds
    • sb!stop Stop the sound
    • sb!join / sb!leave Join / leave voice
    • sb!volume <0-200> Set volume (%)
    • sb!help Show all commands

    Tip: use a regular voice channel. Avoid "join-to-create" channels since the bot can get moved automatically. The bot leaves on its own after a few minutes of inactivity.
